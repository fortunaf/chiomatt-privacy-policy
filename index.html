CHIOMATT Marketplace

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

CHIOMATT Marketplace is a campus marketplace designed to connect University of Nairobi students with products and sellers. We respect your privacy and aim to protect your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Email address for account registration and communication.
  • Password credentials for secure account access, handled through the app's authentication system.
  • Delivery location to help fulfil orders.
  • Order details to manage purchases and deliveries.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to manage accounts, process orders, coordinate deliveries, communicate order updates and improve the marketplace.

4. Payment After Delivery

CHIOMATT operates on a Pay After Delivery model. Customers pay for their orders when products are delivered. Payment information is collected only if a payment feature in the app requires it.

5. Sharing Information

Relevant order and delivery details may be shared with sellers, delivery personnel and service providers when necessary to complete orders and operate the app. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the services and applicable legal obligations. Users may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of their information. Requests are subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Third-Party Services

Third-party providers may process information when necessary to support authentication, hosting and app operations. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
